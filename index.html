Privacy Policy

Find The Croc · Last updated: September 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Find The Croc (“the App”) handles information. We keep things simple: the App does not track you and does not sell your data.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

Game progress (for example, unlocked levels or settings) may be stored only on your device so you can continue playing. That data stays on your device unless you uninstall the App or clear its data.

2. No Tracking

We do not use advertising trackers, analytics SDKs for user profiling, or cross-app tracking. The App does not follow you across other apps or websites.

3. Third-Party Services

If the App opens a web page inside a built-in browser view, that page is provided by a third party and may have its own privacy policy. We do not control how those third parties handle data on their sites.

4. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through a third-party page linked from the App, please contact that service directly.

5. Data Security

Because gameplay data is stored locally on your device, its security depends on your device settings and operating system protections.

6. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us through the App Store / Google Play listing for Find The Croc, or the support email shown on that listing.
